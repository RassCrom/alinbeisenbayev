The 2026 Asian Games ended on 4 October in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan. Forty-six teams took part in the 20th Games, among them a refugee team competing for the first time, and 40 of them won at least one medal. With 469 events across 43 sports, it was the largest programme the Games have had.

## China, then everyone else

China won the Games with 169 gold medals, more than twice as many as the hosts. That is 36% of all the golds on offer. Japan won 83, a national record, and South Korea finished third with 39, fewer than half of Japan's total. Between them the three East Asian teams took 291 of the 470 gold medals.

## Central Asia

Among the Central Asian countries, Uzbekistan finished fifth in the gold-medal table with 21 golds. Kazakhstan finished eighth with 12.

The gold count hides how close Kazakhstan came. By total medals it was fifth, with 84 podium finishes, one fewer than India. But 48 of those 84 were bronze, more than half. Kazakh athletes kept reaching the podium and stopping one or two steps short of the top. The country clearly struggles to turn its potential into wins, and that is not the only problem its sport has.

The rest of the region was further back. Tajikistan finished 22nd, Kyrgyzstan 26th, and Turkmenistan 31st without a single gold medal. Taken together, though, the five countries won 186 medals. As one team, Central Asia would have been third in the medal count, behind only China and Japan.

## Small teams, many golds

Some smaller teams turned a modest haul into a lot of gold. The UAE won 13 medals and 7 of them were gold, the best ratio of the Games. Bahrain won 22 medals, 11 of them gold, enough for tenth place in the table.

## Off the field

The organisation drew complaints from the start: double-booked rooms, buses that never came or went to the wrong venue, and athletes waiting up to 12 hours to register. At one ice hockey game the North Korean anthem was played for the South Korean team, and the head of the South Korean delegation later gave the organisers an "F".

The next Asian Games will be held in Doha in 2030.
